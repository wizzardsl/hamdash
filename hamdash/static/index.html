      Card settings

      Station

      Callsign lookup

      Automatic needs no account: callook and HamDB between them cover US and Canadian callsigns for free. Anywhere else needs an account — a free HamQTH one is enough. QRZ's XML interface is a paid add-on; without it QRZ refuses the session and hamdash shows you exactly what it said. LoTW is a QSL confirmation service, not a callsign directory — it has no public lookup, so it can't fill your grid in.

      Logbook sync

      Save credentials before syncing. Pulling never duplicates what you already have — a matching contact is updated with whatever the other log knows, so confirmations land on the QSO they belong to. LoTW uploads must be signed by TQSL, which holds your certificate; a password cannot stand in for it, so "Push to LoTW" hands your log to TQSL if it is installed and tells you what to do if it isn't.

      Radio

      Radio changes take effect when HamDash restarts.
